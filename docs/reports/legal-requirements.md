# Designing for Swedish AB compliance: what the law forces and what APIs allow

**Scope and disclaimer.** This is research for software design, not legal or tax advice. It was compiled on 2026-10-22 from research notes that read some primary texts in full and relied on search summaries or vendor guides for the rest. Each claim carries a grade: **[P]** read directly from a primary source (statute, BFN general advice, or a Skatteverket page fetched in full), **[S]** secondary only (vendor blog, FAR summary, news, search snippet), **[U]** unverified, from background knowledge or inference. Verify every [S] and [U] item before it becomes product logic.

**Bottom line.** A self-hosted bookkeeping system for a small AB is legally permitted, with no software certification regime found in the law or BFNAR 2013:2 (an absence-of-evidence finding, not confirmed by Skatteverket or BFN). The burden of proof moves to the company, and its main evidence is the systemdokumentation. The design is shaped by five hard constraints. (1) Posted ledger data must be append-only, with corrections made as new entries stating who and when, and with a verifiable gap-free voucher series. (2) Records must stay in Sweden, or in the EU if the location is notified to Skatteverket, for seven years after the calendar year in which the financial year ended, in a form that can be printed immediately. (3) Since 1 July 2024 paper originals may be destroyed after scanning if the transfer carries no risk of change or loss. (4) VAT, payroll (AGI) and INK2 each have a Skatteverket API, but all three stop at a draft that a human with the right role must sign in Skatteverket's e-service. (5) Whether a single AB can get production API access for its own filings is **unresolved**, so the system must work end to end with file export plus manual upload as its baseline and treat the APIs as an upgrade. The annual report has a Bolagsverket iXBRL route that also ends in BankID signing. The most important unknowns are listed in priority order in section 7.

## 1. Bookkeeping law: the ledger must be append-only, gap-free and documented

### Vouchers, posting timeliness and numbering

Every business event needs a verifikation ([BFL 5 kap. 6 §](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/); [P], read via a fetch tool's excerpts rather than the full statute text). Under 5 kap. 7 § it must carry: the date it was compiled; the date of the event; what the event concerns; the amount; the counterparty; where relevant, a reference to underlying documents and where they are kept; a voucher number or other identifier; and anything else needed to link voucher to booking without difficulty. Per BFNAR 2013:2 5.4 these fields must be durable, meaning not erasable or made illegible ([BFNAR 2013:2 consolidated](https://www.bfn.se/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/bfnar13-2-kons2.pdf); [P]). Compile date and event date are therefore separate fields. If the company received a document (a supplier invoice), that document can serve as the voucher, but any completion must not alter the received content and must show when, by whom and what was added (BFNAR 5.8 [P]). Voucher series must be unbroken and "det ska i bokföringssystemet gå att kontrollera att verifikationsserien är obruten" (BFNAR 5.9-5.10 [P]); multiple series are allowed if documented in the systemdokumentation (BFNAR 9.6 [P]).

Timing is set by 5 kap. 2 § and BFNAR ch. 3 [P]. Cash payments must be booked by the next working day, and other events "så snart det kan ske". Booking may be deferred only if vouchers are kept in order meanwhile: up to 50 days after the end of the event month; up to 50 days after quarter-end if normal annual net turnover is at most SEK 3m; up to 60 days after year-end if turnover is at most SEK 1m with at most 50 vouchers or 250 events a year (BFNAR 3.6-3.8 [P]). These are allowances rather than design targets, so the system should post as documents arrive and use the deferral only as a tolerance in its overdue-posting warnings.

### Double entry, corrections and behandlingshistorik

BFL 5 kap. 1 § requires each transaction to be presented both in registreringsordning (journal) and systematisk ordning (ledger) [P]. Each booking record needs registration order, accounting period, voucher identifier, account coding and amount (BFNAR 2.3 [P]). Double-entry itself is a practical consequence of these rules rather than a quoted rule (inference [U]). In computer-based books a correction must be a separate rättelsepost, which is itself a new posting backed by a voucher, and BFL 5 kap. 5 § requires stating when and by whom ([BFL](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/); BFNAR 2.17-2.18, 5.15-5.16 [P]). A replaced voucher remains räkenskapsinformation, and the original data must stay apparent. Books must be reconciled regularly under fixed routines, with deviations documented (BFNAR 2.15-2.16 [P]). BFL 5 kap. 11 § requires both systemdokumentation and behandlingshistorik so every entry can be traced. Behandlingshistorik must show the postings added with registration date and must describe changes to the bookkeeping system that affect how postings are treated, with the date each change took effect (BFNAR 9.15-9.16 [P]). In practice that means versioned posting rules and VAT logic.

### Systemdokumentation

BFNAR 9.2 requires a kontoplan, a description of how accounts roll up into the annual accounts if not obvious, a samlingsplan (system structure) and an arkivplan [P]. The simplified exemption is lost when the system uses automatic postings: "Används automatkonteringar i bokföringsprogrammet anses bokföringssystemet inte vara enkelt" (BFNAR 9.5 [P]). An agentic system that auto-posts therefore owes the full set: posting rules (9.9), information flows across computer-based parts (9.11), the procedure for converting stored data to readable form (9.12), the verifieringskedja from voucher through journal and ledger to the annual accounts and back (9.8), and where behandlingshistorik is created (9.15). The term in the general advice is "arkivplan" (8.3), not "arkivbeskrivning". An AI agent that proposes postings is, in this reading, a "behandlingsregel" generator, so rule and model changes should be logged as system changes (design inference [U]).

### Retention, location and scanning

The text in force from 1 July 2024 (SFS 2024:342, which amended 4 kap. 1 and 4 §§ and 7 kap. 1-7 §§) requires electronic records to be printable by "omedelbar utskrift" and kept "i det format och med det innehåll de hade" when received or compiled. They must be "varaktiga och lätt åtkomliga", kept through the seventh year after the calendar year in which the financial year ended, "i Sverige, i ett ordnat skick och på ett betryggande och överskådligt sätt", with the equipment and systems needed to print them available in Sweden throughout ([SFS 2024:342](https://svenskforfattningssamling.se/sites/default/files/sfs/2024-05/SFS2024-342.pdf); [P]). A 2026 financial year is therefore kept through 31 December 2033 (inference [U]). Electronic storage in another EU country is allowed if the location and every change of it is notified to Skatteverket, immediate electronic access is given on request, and printout in Sweden remains immediately possible (7 kap. 3 a §); Skatteverket can permit other arrangements (7 kap. 4 §) [P]. BFNAR 8.1-8.2 requires backups, kept separate from the copied information [P]. Encryption is allowed only if the information can be shown in clear text immediately (BFNAR 7.8 [P]).

Receipt digitization is the biggest recent liberalisation. 7 kap. 6 § lets the company destroy paper originals once the information is transferred to another document, provided the transfer, "med hänsyn till tekniska metoder, organisatoriska åtgärder och övriga omständigheter", does not risk the information changing or disappearing ([SFS 2024:342](https://svenskforfattningssamling.se/sites/default/files/sfs/2024-05/SFS2024-342.pdf); [P]). BFNAR 7.6 requires that the link between the original and the booking stays traceable [P]. Neither the law nor BFNAR sets DPI or file-format thresholds [P, absence]. Claims that the former minimum three-year paper retention is gone come from [Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/foretagsredovisning/arkivering-bokforing), [FAR Balans](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/balans/2024/maj/balans_2024053001/) and [Pleo](https://blog.pleo.io/sv/digitalisera-kvitton) [S] and are consistent with the statute text, but the BFNAR text read predates the 2024 change (it is consolidated through BFNAR 2017:6) and some cross-references may be stale. A defensible scanning workflow, which is our design inference [U], documents the "organisatoriska åtgärder": legibility check of both sides, no post-scan editing, linkage to the voucher number, immutable storage and backup, and a written procedure in the systemdokumentation. Self-created e-vouchers and received e-invoices should be stored in the format in which they were received.

### Foreign currency, cash method and consequences

Foreign-currency events convert at the spot rate on the event date, with permitted approximations including the rate used under the VAT act (BFNAR 2.4-2.5 [P]). Kontantmetoden (booking on payment) is in BFL 5 kap. 2 § for companies with normal annual net turnover up to SEK 3m, and the fetched statute text did not itself exclude aktiebolag, but a [FAR Rättserien](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/k/rr_kontantmetoden/) summary says it applies only to those not required to prepare an annual report, which would exclude an AB [S, conflicting]. Because an AB must in any case produce an accrual-based annual report, the design should default to accrual and treat kontantmetod as unresolved (see section 7).

Board and CEO carry the responsibility. A Supreme Court judgment (B 1981-16) is summarised as citing the board's duty to organise control of bookkeeping and the VD's duty to ensure bookkeeping complies with law, with liability for bokföringsbrott ([HD B 1981-16](https://www.domstol.se/globalassets/filer/domstol/hogstadomstolen/avgoranden/2017/b-1981-16.pdf); [S], via search summary, ABL paragraph numbers unconfirmed). Bokföringsbrott under 11 kap. 5 § brottsbalken is summarised as up to two years' prison, with a petty grade and a gross grade of six months to six years, and it covers negligence as well as intent ([Ekobrottsmyndigheten](https://www.ekobrottsmyndigheten.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/arf-2023-1-bokforingsbrott.pdf); [S]). Only cash registers need certification (SFL 39 kap. 8 §, referenced in BFNAR 1.12 [P]), which is relevant only if the AB sells over a till.

## 2. VAT: reverse charge is the daily workload, and the new API still ends at a human signature

### Periods, deadlines and registration

Filing frequency depends on turnover: up to SEK 1m annual, quarterly or monthly; up to SEK 40m quarterly or monthly; above that monthly only ([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html); [P]). Monthly and quarterly returns for businesses up to SEK 40m are due on the 12th of the second month after the period, with the 17th in August and January, and payment must be booked on the skattekonto by the same date [P]. A late return costs **SEK 625** [P]; further escalation tiers were not confirmed. Annual-period ABs that file an income tax return report VAT with it; ABs with EU trade file by the 26th of the second month after period end [P]. Skatteverket's page says the invoice method is required above SEK 3m turnover with the cash method optional below it [P]; this is the VAT cash method (bokslutsmetoden) and is distinct from the BFL question above. A Skatteverket summary notes that new rules from 1 July 2026 widen its ability to deny or remove VAT registrations ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms); [S], not read in detail). Skattetillägg for incorrect VAT is cited as 20% of the understated tax ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/skatter/betalaochfatillbaka/skattetillaggforforetag.4.2cf1b5cd163796a5c8bc24e.html); [S] via summary).

### Reverse charge on foreign SaaS

Skatteverket's page on reverse charge [P] states that services bought from another EU country go in ruta 21 and services from outside the EU in ruta 22. The self-assessed output VAT goes in ruta 30, 31 or 32 depending on rate and the deductible input VAT in ruta 48, and both sides must be shown with no netting ([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/omvandskattskyldighet.4.47eb30f51122b1aaad28000258292.html)). If the foreign supplier wrongly charged VAT, reverse charge still applies to the full amount, the wrong VAT cannot be deducted, and the buyer should request a credit note [P]. Which entity invoiced decides EU versus non-EU, so the system must capture the invoicing entity's country and VAT number per supplier, not per brand (inference [U]; the notes did not verify the invoicing entities of AWS, Google, GitHub or Atlassian). Sales of services to EU businesses go in ruta 39 and in the periodisk sammanställning with the buyer's VAT number ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagorganisationer/moms/momsvidhandelmedeulander/momsvidinternationelltjanstehandel.4.76a43be412206334b89800010696.html); [P] via search summary). Non-EU service sales to ruta 40, the full ruta list, the rate-to-box mapping for 30/31/32 and the BAS reverse-charge accounts (2614/2645 pattern, 4535/4531, 3308/3305) are all [U] and must be checked against the current SKV 4700 and BAS 2026.

### Invoice content and deductions

A simplified invoice is allowed up to SEK 4,000 including VAT, but not where the buyer must reverse-charge ([FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/f/rr_forenkladfaktura/), [Bokio](https://www.bokio.se/blogg/forenklad-faktura/); [S]). The full list of mandatory invoice fields (ML 17 kap. 24 §) was not retrieved [U]. Meal representation is deductible for VAT on a base of at most SEK 300 ex VAT per person and occasion, as is gift VAT ([FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rm_representation/), [Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/representation); [S]). Bad-debt relief is claimed in the return for the period in which the loss is confirmed, using the original invoice ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/kundforlusteromkundenintekanbetala.4.5c1163881590be297b58d10.html); [S] via summary). A filed return is corrected by resubmitting the whole return ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/rattaenmomsdeklaration.4.3684199413c956649b552c4.html); [S] via summary; the six-year window comes from [Bokio](https://www.bokio.se/hjalp/moms-skatt/moms/ratta-momsdeklaration/) [S]).

### Filing channels

Three routes exist: manual entry in the e-service "Lämna momsdeklaration", XML upload in the same e-service, and the API Momsdeklaration. The API went live in November 2025 and Fortnox connected in February 2026; it creates and reads return data in the company's own space, while final signing happens in Skatteverket's e-service ([Fortnox](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/driva-foretag/skatt/forenkla-momsdeklarationen-med-skatteverkets-nya-api); [P] for the Fortnox page content, which is a vendor's description of Skatteverket's service). Skatteverket lists a "komplett testtjänst" and a production tier, but the portal page is partly stale ([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/utvecklingsomraden/moms.4.7c708f0e16bed42cd0553bd.html); [P]). The XML upload format (eSKD or other) was not confirmed [U].

## 3. Payroll and AGI: one monthly XML, reconciled against Skatteverket's own calculation

### What the system must produce

AGI is a monthly XML file with a huvuduppgift (employer totals) and one individuppgift per payee, due the month after payout, with a zero declaration required if nothing is paid ([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/lamnaarbetsgivardeklaration/narskajaglamnaarbetsgivardeklaration.4.361dc8c15312eff6fd13c11.html); [P]). Technical description 1.1.18.2 is the only version listed. On the huvuduppgift, FK487 (total employer contributions and särskild löneskatt) is checked against Skatteverket's own calculation from the individuppgifter, FK497 is total tax withheld, and further fields cover deductions such as R&D and regional support. Each individuppgift needs FK570 (unique specification number), one identity field, FK011 for taxable cash pay subject to contributions, and exactly one of seven tax fields, of which FK001 is preliminary tax withheld (value 0 if nothing was withheld). Benefit fields include FK013 (car benefit) and FK018 (fuel benefit, market value times 1.2) ([Skatteverket tech. description](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/lamnaarbetsgivardeklaration/tekniskbeskrivningochtesttjanst/tekniskbeskrivning1116.4.7eada0316ed67d7282a791.html); [P], read as a page-text summary, with full element names in an XSD attachment not retrieved). A corrected individuppgift with the same period, identity and specification number replaces the original; removal uses FK205 and a mismatched specification number creates a duplicate [P]. The system should store the specification number permanently per person and period, recompute contributions per person, and require that the sum of FK001 equals FK497 before export (design inference [U]).

### Rates and 2026 limits

Contribution rates for 2026 come only from a secondary source: **31.42%** for those born 1959 or later, 10.21% for those born 1938-1958, 0% for 1937 or earlier, särskild löneskatt on pension costs 24.26%, prisbasbelopp SEK 59,200 and inkomstbasbelopp SEK 83,400 ([Fortnox](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/driva-foretag/skatt/belopp-och-procentsatser); [S], the primary Skatteverket PDF could not be parsed). A temporary youth reduction for employees born 2003-2007 applies from 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2027 on pay up to SEK 25,000 a month, giving 20.81% in 2026 ([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/omoss/pressochmedia/nyheter/2026/nyheter/lagrearbetsgivaravgifterforungdomar.5.70685bee19c85dd5dd02b10.html); [P] for existence and dates; the AGI reporting mechanism was not found). From 2026 the växa-stöd reduction is no longer netted in AGI but claimed afterwards via a separate form within a year ([Företagarna](https://www.foretagarna.se/nyheter/riks/2025/oktober/vaxa-stodet-blir-krangligare-fran-arsskiftet/); [S]). Tax-free and benefit values for 2026, all [S] from [Fortnox](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/driva-foretag/skatt/belopp-och-procentsatser): kostförmån SEK 62 / 124 / 310; domestic traktamente SEK 300 per full day; mileage SEK 25 per mil for a private car; Christmas gift SEK 600; friskvård SEK 5,000. These must be configurable year tables rather than constants.

### Owner-director salary

Under the new 3:12 rules from income year 2026, grundbelopp is four inkomstbasbelopp of the prior year (SEK 322,400 for 2026), the lönebaserat utrymme is 50% of (löneunderlag times ownership share minus 8 inkomstbasbelopp), and the old lönekrav and förenklingsregel are replaced ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/foretagsformer/famansforetag/andradereglerinforinkomstdeklarationen2027.4.4a54dc8b19aa6175a152359.html); [P]). The product should report löneunderlag and not advise on salary mix. K10 belongs to the owner's personal return and is a separate filing the system can only support with data.

### Penalties, ledger mapping and channels

Late AGI costs SEK 625 per Skatteverket [P]; secondary sources add a SEK 1,250 tier for repeated lateness or ignored orders, with the conflict unresolved ([Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/glomt-skattedeklarationen), [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rl_arbetsgivardeklaration/); [S]). Standard BAS postings are debit 7210 (or 7010/7220) and 7510; credit 1930, 2710 and 2731; settlement against 1630 ([Fortnox](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/bokforingstips/loneutbetalning); [S]); pension account numbers are [U]. AGI can be entered in the e-service, uploaded as XML (UTF-8, root attribute `omrade="Arbetsgivardeklaration"`, up to 1,000 declarations per upload file) or sent via API; a public test service validates files with no login ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/lamnaarbetsgivardeklaration/tekniskbeskrivningochtesttjanst.4.309a41aa1672ad0c8377c8b.html); [P]). In the API flow the file sits in a staging area until an authorised person signs it in the e-service ([service description](https://www7.skatteverket.se/portal-wapi/open/apier-och-oppna-data/utvecklarportalen/v1/getFile/tjanstebeskrivning-agd-inlamning-v1); [S], not opened directly; versions cited as 1.7 and 1.8 in different notes).

## 4. Year-end: K2 versus K3, iXBRL to Bolagsverket and INK2 via SRU

### Framework and audit

Updated K-rules apply for financial years starting after 31 December 2025, and K2 excludes more companies from FY2026, including those with share-based compensation, foreign branches, some crypto holdings, and those whose buildings are at least 75% of net turnover ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html); [S]). A public AB cannot use K2 ([FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/bokforingsnamnden/bfnar/bfnar20160010/); [S]). The size thresholds are unresolved: a reproduced ÅRL text gives 50 employees, SEK 40m balance sheet and SEK 80m turnover for "större företag" ([FAR](https://www.faronline.se/link/9d842582db7b407e85d61fcc0a92aae2.aspx); [S], possibly outdated). Audit is not compulsory below 3 employees, SEK 1.5m balance sheet and SEK 3m turnover, with the usual formulation being that audit duty arises when more than one limit is exceeded in two consecutive years ([Bokio](https://www.bokio.se/ordlista-for-foretagare/revision/); [S]; [Riksdag motion](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/GX01CU28.html) wording is ambiguous). The notes did not retrieve the statute text for ÅRL or ABL 9 kap. 1 §. The required components of a K2 annual report (förvaltningsberättelse, resultaträkning, balansräkning, noter) are [U], from memory.

### Annual report deadlines, fees and format

The annual report is due at Bolagsverket within seven months of year-end (31 July for a calendar year). Late fees for financial years from 2025 are cited as SEK 7,500 for a private AB, a second SEK 7,500 after more than two months and a third level after more than four months (amount unconfirmed; one summary says SEK 15,000 for a private AB) ([FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/fakta-och-siffror/fs_foerseningsavgift_aarsredovisning/); [S]). If not filed within 11 months Bolagsverket must order liquidation (ABL 25 kap. 11 §; [S] via search summary). Bolagsverket takes annual reports as iXBRL, and the machine-to-machine "Lämna in årsredovisning digitalt" interface is reported to need no agreement, with a contact form as next step ([Bolagsverket](https://bolagsverket.se/apierochoppnadata/lamnaforetagsinformation/apifordigitalinlamningavarsredovisningochrevisionsberattelse/anslutningtilltjanstenlamnainarsredovisningendigitalt.5936.html); [S] via search snippet, as bolagsverket.se blocked direct fetching). Technical documents exist (iXBRL application guide v1.8, technical framework v1.2, notification and file formats v2.2) and K2 taxonomy and example files are free at [taxonomier.se](https://taxonomier.se/faq.html) [S]. Other [S] sources say vendor software contracted with Bolagsverket is needed, which conflicts with the no-agreement claim ([Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/sv-se/expert-insights/fakta-om-digital-inlamning-av-arsredovisning), [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/ratt-bolagsratt/d/rb_digitalarsredovisning/)). All board members and any auditor sign before filing, and the fastställelseintyg signer uses personal BankID [S]. Digital filing is not mandatory according to the sources found: a 2023 ministry memo proposed it from 1 January 2026, but SOU 2026:26 consultation and a June 2026 government assignment suggest it has slipped ([regeringen.se](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/d80917262ab14de5bbff617fcbda2b2c/digital-ingivning-av-arsredovisningar.pdf); [SRF konsulterna](https://www.srfkonsult.se/globalassets/content/dokument/remisser/2026/srf-konsulterna-remissvar-sou-2026-26-digitala-verktyg-inom-bolagsratten-ju2026-01028.pdf); [S]). Paper filing remains possible ([RSM](https://www.rsm.global/sweden/sv/news/digital-signering-och-inlamning-av-arsredovisning); [S]). Bolagsverket also offers a free read API, "Värdefulla datamängder", launched 3 February 2025 (OAuth2, 60 requests per minute), useful for fetching company data but not for submission ([Bolagsverket](https://bolagsverket.se/apierochoppnadata/hamtaforetagsinformation/vardefulladatamangder/apiforvardefulladatamangder.5513.html); [S]).

### INK2 and SRU

For a calendar-year AB the FY2025 INK2 opened 2 February 2026 and was due 3 August 2026 ([Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/foretagsdeklaration/deklarationsdatum), [Azets](https://www.azets.com/sv-se/resurser/blogg/bolagets-inkomstdeklaration-viktiga-datum-och-vanliga-fallgropar); [S]; Dagens PS gave 1 August, a conflict explained by 1 August 2026 being a Saturday). The FY2026 return will be due in 2027 and its date was not found. Skatteverket's file transfer takes INK1 and INK2 as a pair of files, info.sru and blanketter.sru (blanketter at most 5 MB), is then signed in Mina sidor, and returns a receipt listing format errors ([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/filoverforing.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d5e.html), [SKV 269](https://skatteverket.se/download/18.4a4d586616058d860bc4a04/1515425653062/SKV269_24.pdf); [P] for the file-transfer page, though the SKV 269 edition read may be outdated). An INK2 API with "hämta" and "inlämning" parts loads data into the company's own space for signing in Mina sidor, needs a signed agreement and Skatteverket approval, and its service description INK2-4 v1.02 was updated 2025-10-24 ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/etjansterochblanketter/allaetjanster/tjanster/inkomstdeklaration2); [S] via search summary). Tax calculation (periodiseringsfond, schablonintäkt, non-deductible items, INK2R and INK2S contents) was not researched, and the corporate tax rate was not sourced.

## 5. Formats, APIs and what can actually be automated

### Formats

SIE types 1-4 are the line-based classic: type 4 adds all vouchers, type 4i is vouchers only for subsystem import, and the current specification is "SIE filformat ver 4C" dated 2025-08-06 ([sie.se](https://sie.se/format/); [S], the 4C PDF text was not extracted). SIE 5 is an XML successor from 2018 with limited support ([sie.se](https://sie.se/format/); [S]). Encoding (CP437 / "PC8"), #IB/#UB/#RES year indexes and #DIM/#OBJEKT are from background knowledge [U]. BAS 2026 is current (PDF dated 14 April 2026, 39 pages, accounts marked # to be avoided under K2) ([bas.se](https://www.bas.se/kontoplaner/); [S]). **BAS licensing for embedding in software is not established**; the licence contact is info@bas.se. Reusable open-source code is thin: an MIT-licensed Rust `sie4` crate (stale since 2023) and an MIT Ruby `barsoom/sie` gem ([lib.rs](https://lib.rs/crates/sie4), [GitHub](https://github.com/barsoom/sie); [S]).

### Can one company get production API access?

Skatteverket's process [P] is: request test access through a form and accept terms, receive client_id and client_secret by email; for production, meet API-specific requirements, sign an agreement, receive credentials split across email and SMS, and register https redirect URIs ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/borjaanvandaapier.4.77f3e230191ef88bf1d585.html); the related [anslutning och support](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/anslutningochsupportforvaraapierochoppnadata.4.7c708f0e16bed42cd055bf0.html) page is [S]). Authentication is OAuth2, with e-legitimation (BankID, with Test BankID for testing) or an organisation certificate, currently accepted only if issued by Expisoft, and the user needs a role such as firmatecknare or registreringsombud [P]. The agreements are drafted for a "programvaruföretag" and Skatteverket must approve each application, which suggests a self-hosted open-source tool cannot assume production keys per deployment [S, inference]. **No source states whether a single company filing only for itself can qualify.** That is an open question to settle by writing to Skatteverket's API support, and the design must not depend on the answer.

### Banking and e-invoicing inputs

Lunar's open-banking API requires eIDAS TPP certificates and OAuth2 ([Lunar docs](https://docs.openbanking.lunar.app/api-overview); [S]). Enable Banking offers a restricted production mode for linking one's own accounts without KYB, but Lunar coverage there is unconfirmed ([Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com/docs/faq); [S]). GoCardless Bank Account Data is reported closed to new signups ([Actual Budget docs](https://actualbudget.org/docs/advanced/bank-sync/gocardless/); [S]). Fallback is file import of bank statements. Public bodies must receive structured e-invoices (Peppol BIS Billing 3) since 1 April 2019, while private B2B Peppol is voluntary ([Försäkringskassan](https://www.forsakringskassan.se/download/18.2807d9c217c1f0156733e0/1680168372496/att-skicka-e-faktura-till-forsakringskassan.pdf); [S]); a self-hosted system would need an access-point provider.

### Requirements checklist

**Must have.** Append-only ledger with no update or delete of posted entries; corrections as linked entries with their own vouchers, user and timestamp. Gap-free numbered voucher series with an integrity check. Separate compile date and event date. Immutable storage of original documents in their received format, with scan-to-voucher linkage and a recorded scanning procedure. Accrual accounting with BAS-based chart and K2/K3 mapping. VAT logic including reverse charge for foreign services, with supplier country captured. Payroll with AGI XML generation to technical description 1.1.18.2 and per-person specification numbers. Year tables for rates and limits. SIE4 export and import. SRU generation for INK2. Backups stored separately, with restore tests. Retention of seven years plus a runtime or readable export for the whole period. Data stored in Sweden, or in the EU with a Skatteverket notice. Full systemdokumentation generated from the system: posting-rule version history, verification chain, information flows, arkivplan. Human approval before any filing, with every agent-proposed posting traceable.

**Nice to have.** VAT API integration (after production access is confirmed). AGI and INK2 API integration. iXBRL generation and submission to Bolagsverket. Bank import through an aggregator or open banking. OCR with human confirmation. Periodisk sammanställning export. SIE5. Peppol access-point integration. Skattekonto reconciliation. Bolagsverket read-API lookups. K10 data export. Hash chains on stored documents. Support for kontantmetod, if confirmed as legal for an AB.

## 7. Open questions and unverified claims, in priority order

1. **Production API access for a single company's own filings (VAT, AGI, INK2).** Agreements are drafted for software companies, and nothing found covers self-use. Ask Skatteverket API support; until answered, design for file export and manual upload.

## Conclusion

The research shows that legality turns on ledger integrity and archive design, which are fully specified in primary sources, while filing automation turns on onboarding with Skatteverket and Bolagsverket, which is not. That asymmetry suggests the build order: first the append-only ledger, documentation generator and archive, then file exports (SIE4, AGI XML, SRU, VAT XML, iXBRL) that are valid by construction and testable against Skatteverket's public AGI test service, and only then the API clients. Every filing route ends at a human signature, so "agentic" bookkeeping here means preparing, checking and explaining filings, not submitting them unattended.

The sharpest risks are not in the law but in the evidence gaps. The bookkeeping law and the 2024 digitization change are well sourced, while most numerical parameters (rates, thresholds, penalty tiers, deadlines for FY2026) rest on secondary sources and must be loaded as versioned, sourced tables that a human re-verifies each year. The two questions that could most change the product, production API access for a single company and Bolagsverket's submission requirements, can each be resolved by one email, and should be sent before architecture decisions are frozen.
