## Which framework applies (K2 vs K3), size thresholds, required components

### Takeaway
Small ABs normally choose K2 (BFNAR 2016:10) or K3 (BFNAR 2012:1); updated K-rules apply from financial years starting after 31 Dec 2025, and K2 has new exclusions from FY2026. Exact ÅRL size thresholds could not be verified from a primary source in this session.

### Cited Findings
- Updated K-regelverk apply for financial years beginning after 31 Dec 2025 — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html) (via search summary)
- A public AB may not apply K2 even if small; a parent preparing published consolidated accounts may not apply K2 — [FAR/BFN search results](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/bokforingsnamnden/bfnar/bfnar20160010/) (via search summary)
- From FY2026 additional companies may not use K2 (K2 points 1.1A–1.1C): per KPMG e.g. bostadsrättsföreningar, companies with foreign branches, companies with share-based compensation, companies with crypto assets (some exceptions), companies whose buildings account for >=75% of net turnover — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html) (secondhand summary; verify against BFN's updated BFNAR 2016:10 consolidated text [bfn.se](https://www.bfn.se/wp-content/uploads/vl16-10-k2ar-kons2025.pdf))
- ÅRL "större företag" test: more than one of three criteria (avg employees >50, balance sheet total >40 MSEK, net turnover >80 MSEK) in each of the two last years — the 50/40/80 figures appear in a reproduced ÅRL version for "större koncern"/större företag; mindre företag = not större. Source reproduced older version; may have been changed — [FAR search result](https://www.faronline.se/link/9d842582db7b407e85d61fcc0a92aae2.aspx). CONFLICT/UNCERTAIN: I suspect the thresholds were raised (EU inflation adjustment) but found no source confirming 25/50 MSEK; do not rely on either set without checking SFS.
- Net turnover definition per ÅRL: income from goods sold and services in normal operations, less discounts, VAT and other taxes directly tied to turnover — [FAR search result](https://www.faronline.se/link/9d842582db7b407e85d61fcc0a92aae2.aspx)

### Inferences
- A typical small AB is "mindre företag" under either threshold set, so K2 (simplified) is generally available unless an exclusion applies; K3 is the alternative (more complete).
- The required components (resultaträkning, balansräkning, noter, förvaltningsberättelse) were not sourced here; see Gaps.

### Gaps
- Primary-source ÅRL 1 kap. thresholds and component list (not retrieved). From training memory only (unverified): K2 annual report = förvaltningsberättelse, resultaträkning, balansräkning, noter; no kassaflödesanalys required for small.
- No source found for the 2026 K2 vs K3 choice criteria beyond the exclusions above.

## Audit duty (revisionsplikt)

### Takeaway
Small ABs are exempt from audit if they stay at or under limits of 3 employees, 1.5 MSEK balance sheet and 3 MSEK net turnover (exceeding more than one criterion for two consecutive years triggers audit). Sources disagree on exact wording; statute not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Limits cited: 3 employees, 1.5 MSEK balance sheet total, 3 MSEK net turnover, each in the two latest financial years — [Riksdag motion/search result](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/GX01CU28.html) (wording ambiguous on "maximalt")
- Bokio: audit duty arises if the AB exceeds two of three limits two years in a row (>3 employees, >1.5 MSEK balance sheet, >3 MSEK turnover); financial companies, public ABs and ABs with special dividend restriction always need an auditor; voluntary audit allowed — [Bokio](https://www.bokio.se/ordlista-for-foretagare/revision/)
- Motion asks government to raise thresholds — change not confirmed — [Riksdag](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/GX01CU28.html)

### Inferences
- Source conflict "maximalt"/"more than" is likely a motion drafting issue; the commonly stated rule is "at least two of three exceeded". Statute text (ABL 9 kap. 1 §) should confirm: I recall "more than one" of the three criteria, i.e. two of three.

### Gaps
- ABL 9 kap. 1 § text not retrieved; whether the 2026 thresholds changed is unconfirmed. Abolition of mandatory audit 2010 (Prop. 2009/10:204 is listed at [lagen.nu](https://lagen.nu/prop/2009/10:204)) – details not read.

## Deadlines, fees, late-filing penalties

### Takeaway
Annual report + audit report due at Bolagsverket within 7 months of year-end (31 July for calendar year). Late fees (FY starting from 1 Jan 2025): 7,500 SEK private AB / 15,000 SEK public, escalating.

### Cited Findings
- Förseningsavgift 1: not filed within 7 months; 15,000 SEK public / 7,500 SEK private — [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/fakta-och-siffror/fs_foerseningsavgift_aarsredovisning/)
- Förseningsavgift 2: more than 2 months late, additional 15,000 / 7,500 SEK — [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/fakta-och-siffror/fs_foerseningsavgift_aarsredovisning/)
- Förseningsavgift 3: >4 months late; one search summary says additional 15,000 SEK private / 30,000 SEK public (FAR table cut off; secondhand) — [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/fakta-och-siffror/fs_foerseningsavgift_aarsredovisning/)
- Prior levels (FY before 2025): 5,000 / 10,000 SEK for first fee — [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/fakta-och-siffror/fs_foerseningsavgift_aarsredovisning/) (via search summary)
- If not filed within 11 months, Bolagsverket shall order liquidation (ABL 25 kap. 11 §) — [search summary of FAR/riksdagen](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-1995-1633.text)

### Inferences
- Cumulative worst case for private AB for FY2025+ is probably 30,000 SEK (7.5+7.5+15), consistent with the amounts above; confirm Fee 3 on Bolagsverket.

### Gaps
- Bolagsverket filing fee (digital vs paper) not found. Annual meeting within 6 months, protocol/fastställelseintyg requirements not sourced (ABL 7 kap. not retrieved).

## Digital filing: format, API, auth, test environment, tooling

### Takeaway
Bolagsverket accepts annual reports as iXBRL (K2/K3 limited companies); filing is available via an e-service and a machine-to-machine "Lämna in årsredovisning digitalt" API requiring no agreement. Digital filing is NOT yet legally mandatory as far as found; the mandate has slipped.

### Cited Findings
- Service built on iXBRL; interface between accounting software and Bolagsverket; no file/doc upload in the service; began 2018, expanded to most K2/K3 ABs — [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/sv-se/expert-insights/fakta-om-digital-inlamning-av-arsredovisning) (secondhand)
- Report must be a complete annual report tagged in iXBRL with a current taxonomy; no agreement required to connect, contact Bolagsverket via form "Kontakta oss om API:er" for next step — [Bolagsverket API page](https://bolagsverket.se/apierochoppnadata/lamnaforetagsinformation/apifordigitalinlamningavarsredovisningochrevisionsberattelse/anslutningtilltjanstenlamnainarsredovisningendigitalt.5936.html) (via search snippet; direct fetch blocked)
- Technical docs: Tillämpningsanvisning iXBRL v1.8 (2023-11-10) [PDF](https://bolagsverket.se/download/18.2733cf65187efcf5c7e5b972/1700048440182/tillampningsanvisningar-arsredovisning-ixbrl-1-8.pdf); Tekniskt ramverk v1.2 [PDF](https://bolagsverket.se/download/18.7e28353618c67d1aad6bcb/1711611730725/tekniskt-ramverk-digital-inlamning-arsredovisning-1.2.pdf); Aviseringar och filformat v2.2 [PDF](https://bolagsverket.se/download/18.6e6c29d18481cff0f21ff/1670312300299/avisering-digital-inlamning-arsredovisning-2.2.pdf); technical guide v3.5 [page](https://bolagsverket.se/omoss/utvecklingavdigitalatjanster/digitalinlamningavarsredovisning/tekniskt dokumentation)
- Taxonomies and K2 examples (iXBRL and XBRL) published at [taxonomier.se](https://taxonomier.se/faq.html); submission has iXBRL (.xhtml), XBRL (.xbrl) and PDF files, audit report suffix _RB — search summary of Bolagsverket docs
- Before filing, all board members and auditor sign (electronically or on paper); person signing fastställelseintyg uses personal BankID — [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/sv-se/expert-insights/fakta-om-digital-inlamning-av-arsredovisning)
- Commercial software supporting digital filing: Wolters Kluwer, Visma, Hogia, Briljant, Björn Lundén — same source
- Paper filing still possible: print signed report plus original paper fastställelseintyg — [RSM](https://www.rsm.global/sweden/sv/news/digital-signering-och-inlamning-av-arsredovisning)
- Mandate: 2023 Justice Ministry memo proposed mandatory digital filing for most ABs from 1 Jan 2026 (excl. listed/IFRS) — [regeringen.se](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/d80917262ab14de5bbff617fcbda2b2c/digital-ingivning-av-arsredovisningar.pdf); not in force per sources: SOU 2026:26 consultation and a June 2026 government assignment to Bolagsverket to investigate a digital service — [SRF konsulterna](https://www.srfkonsult.se/globalassets/content/dokument/remisser/2026/srf-konsulterna-remissvar-sou-2026-26-digitala-verktyg-inom-bolagsratten-ju2026-01028.pdf); Realtid July 2026 reports Bolagsverket wants mandatory, business groups oppose — [Realtid](https://www.realtid.se/bors-finans/bolagsverket-en-analog-idiot-loop/) (opinion piece, one source says reports are "produced digitally but sent on paper")
- Separate read API: REST/JSON retrieval of annual reports (iXBRL from 2020 onward) — [Bolagsverket](https://bolagsverket.se/apierochoppnadata/hamtaforetagsinformation/apiforatthamtaforetagsinformation.3988.html) (snippet)

### Inferences
- Free tooling: taxonomy and example files on taxonomier.se are free; no free end-to-end open-source iXBRL generator was found. For open-accounting software, plan to generate iXBRL against the Swedish taxonomy and submit via the API.
- The API submission step likely ends with signing by BankID by board members (via Bolagsverket's signing/notification flow); not verified.

### Gaps
- Authentication method (certificate/OAuth?), test environment existence and URL for the Bolagsverket submission API: not found (pages blocked). Contact route: the "Kontakta oss om API:er" form. Whether the mandatory regime is in force and its date: unresolved (conflicting/unclear dates; check regeringen.se/riksdagen.se). Digital filing fee not found.

## INK2 tax return: forms, deadlines, submission, API/SRU

### Takeaway
INK2 for a calendar-year AB is due 3 August 2026 (form available 2 Feb); it can be submitted via e-service, SRU file transfer (then signed on Mina sidor), or the INK2 API used by software vendors.

### Cited Findings
- Calendar-year companies: INK2 opens 2 Feb 2026; last day 3 Aug 2026 regardless of method; extra tax payment 12 Feb (deficit >30,000 SEK) / 4 May (<=30,000); final tax 15 Dec; remaining tax due 12 Mar 2027 — [Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/foretagsdeklaration/deklarationsdatum); 3 Aug also confirmed by [Azets](https://www.azets.com/sv-se/resurser/blogg/bolagets-inkomstdeklaration-viktiga-datum-och-vanliga-fallgropar)
- CONFLICT: Dagens PS gives 1 Aug; a FAR table lists 1 July for one period group — [Dagens PS](https://www.dagensps.se/foretag/eget-foretag-har-ar-arets-viktiga-datum-for-din-deklaration/). 3 Aug (1 Aug is a Saturday in 2026) is best supported. The "1 July / 1 Feb" in the brief were not confirmed.
- File transfer service accepts INK1 and INK2 with attachments; files info.sru and blanketter.sru (<5 MB); declaration then signed on Mina sidor; receipt lists format errors; SKV 269 describes format and a test function — [Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/filoverforing.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d5e.html), [SRU info](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/etjansterochblanketter/allaetjanster/tjanster/filoverforing/informationomsruuppgifter.4.3dfca4f410f4fc63c86800020896.html), [SKV 269 ed. 24](https://skatteverket.se/download/18.4a4d586616058d860bc4a04/1515425653062/SKV269_24.pdf) (edition may be outdated)
- INK2 API: separate "hämta" (pre-filled data as PDF/SRU, status) and "inlämning" APIs; upload to protected own space; not received until signed; software deep-links to Mina sidor where signatory (registered with Bolagsverket) or deklarationsombud signs with e-ID; API consumers use Client ID/Secret; organisation certificate; signed agreement and Skatteverket approval required; service description INK2-4 v1.02 updated 2025-10-24 — [Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/etjansterochblanketter/allaetjanster/tjanster/inkomstdeklaration2) (via search summary), [service description](https://www7.skatteverket.se/portal-wapi/open/apier-och-oppna-data/utvecklarportalen/v1/getFile/tjanstebeskrivning-inkomstdeklaration2-4-v1-02), [avtal](https://www7.skatteverket.se/portal-wapi/open/apier-och-oppna-data/utvecklarportalen/v1/getFile/avtal-ink2api-inlamning-v1)
- Skatteverket test environment concept: separate from production, access by connection request — [Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/omvaraapier/avtalsprocessforpartnerapier/anvandarvillkorforoppetapiitestmiljo.4.515a6be615c637b9aa4145ca.html) (generic for open APIs; INK2-specific sandbox unconfirmed)
- INK2 attachments reach Mina sidor as PDFs; only main form shown for signing — [Skatteverket QA](https://skatteverket.se/download/18.109dcbe71721adafd2517f8/1591259144299/QA%20Underlätta%20för%20företagare%20INK2.pdf)

### Inferences
- A fully open (no-agreement) path does not exist for Skatteverket; an open-source tool can generate SRU files (INK2, INK2R, INK2S) for manual upload or Mina sidor, which is the lowest-friction route.

### Gaps
- Tax calculation specifics (periodiseringsfond up to 25%, schablonintäkt, non-deductible items), INK2R/INK2S content, tax rate: not researched here. Extension deadlines for companies (anstånd) not found; 1 Feb extension unconfirmed. F-skatt/preliminary tax payment mechanics not sourced beyond extra-payment dates above.

## K10 for owners of fåmansbolag

### Takeaway
K10 is part of the owner's personal return (INK1), due 4 May 2026 (1 June / 15 June with anstånd/byråanstånd). 2025 income year is the last under old 3:12 rules; new rules from 1 Jan 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Deadline 4 May 2026, 1 June with anstånd, 15 June byråanstånd — [Azets](https://www.azets.com/sv-se/resurser/blogg/deklaration-2025-for-famansforetag)
- New 3:12 rules from 1 Jan 2026; förenklingsregeln abolished from income year 2026; grundbelopp for 2026 = 4 inkomstbasbelopp = 322,400 SEK — [Driva Eget](https://driva-eget.se/artiklar/nya-regler-for-k10-2026), [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/ratt-skatt/a2/rs_aretsgransbelopp/) (secondhand)
- Skatteverket brochure SKV 292 ed. 34 covers rules for income year 2025 — [Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/download/18.1522bf3f19aea8075ba4f3e/1768831040199/skatteregler-for-delagare-i-famansforetag-skv292-utgava34.pdf) (contents not read)

### Inferences
- For a 2026 fiscal year (filed spring 2027) the new gränsbelopp rules apply; K10 changes need a fresh check.
