## Filing deadlines, periods and thresholds

### Takeaway
Period depends on taxable turnover (beskattningsunderlag): up to SEK 1M annual/quarterly/monthly; up to 40M quarterly or monthly; over 40M monthly only. The "12th" rule applies to most small businesses (12th of the second month after period end, 17th in January and August); the 26th applies to annual periods of AB with EU trade and to monthly filers over SEK 40M. Payment must be booked on the skattekonto by the same date.

### Cited Findings
- Thresholds: up to SEK 1M turnover can choose annual, quarterly or monthly; up to SEK 40M quarterly or monthly; over SEK 40M monthly only. A change to less frequent filing is possible only after 24 months. — [Skatteverket, När ska jag deklarera moms?](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html) (fetched directly)
- Monthly, turnover up to SEK 40M: due 12th of the second month after the period (17th in January and August). Monthly, over SEK 40M (excl. EU acquisitions/imports): 26th of the month after the period (27th in December). — same source
- Quarterly: 12th of the second month after period end, 17th in August; Jan-Mar is due 12 May. — same source. Secondary list of 2026 dates: 12 Feb (Q4 2025), 12 May, 12 Aug (but see conflict), 12 Nov — [Fortnox datumguide via search summary](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/driva-foretag/skatt/datum-skatter-och-moms); contradicted on August by Skatteverket (17 August). Treat Skatteverket as authoritative; 17 Aug 2026 is a Monday, 12 Aug is a Wednesday (calendar fact), so check the official "Viktiga datum".
- Annual period, AB without EU trade that files an income tax return: VAT filed/paid together with the income tax return. Deadlines by fiscal year end: Jan-Apr: 12 Nov (paper) / 12 Dec (digital); May-Jun: 27 Dec / 17 Jan; Jul-Aug: 12 Mar / 12 Apr; Sep-Dec: 12 Jul / 17 Aug. — same Skatteverket page
- AB with EU trade, or that does not file an income tax return, on an annual period: 26th of the second month after period end (27th in December). — same source
- VAT must be both paid and booked on the skattekonto by the filing deadline. — same source
- Accounting method: invoice method (faktureringsmetoden) required above SEK 3M annual turnover; cash method (bokslutsmetoden) optional up to SEK 3M. — same source
- Extension to file needs special reasons (illness, serious event) and does not extend payment. — same source
- A zero return cannot be filed when there are amounts to report. — same source
- New VAT rules from 1 July 2026 give Skatteverket greater ability to check and in some cases deny or remove a VAT registration. — [Skatteverket moms](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms) (via search summary only; not read in detail)
- Periodisk sammanställning for EU service sales is filed via e-service, file transfer or form SKV 5740. — [Björn Lundén/Skatteverket via search](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/etjansterochblanketter/allaetjanster/tjanster/periodisksammanstallningformoms.4.7856a2b411550b99fb7800086478.html)

### Inferences
- A typical small IT AB with turnover below SEK 1M can elect annual, but with EU service sales or reverse-charge activity, annual means the 26th-of-second-month rule rather than combining with the income tax return. Most small consultancies choose quarterly or monthly for cash-flow reasons.
- The task's "12th/26th rule": 12th is the main rule for turnover <= SEK 40M; 26th is the exception (see above).

## Rates, registration, invoice requirements

### Takeaway
Standard rate 25%, with reduced 12% and 6%, and 0%. Food sold in shops/takeaway is temporarily 6% from 1 April 2026 to 31 Dec 2027; restaurant/cafe on-premises stays 12%. Simplified invoice allowed up to SEK 4,000 incl. VAT.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 April 2026 food is 6% VAT; on-premises restaurant/cafe sales remain 12%; takeaway is 6%; temporary through 31 December 2027. — [Skatteverket via search summary](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80002497.html); [Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/matmoms) (secondary)
- Simplified invoice (förenklad faktura) permitted when total incl. VAT <= SEK 4,000 (ML 17 kap. 28 §). It may not be used where the seller is not established in the country where VAT is due and the buyer pays under reverse charge. A Riksdag document mentions SEK 2,000, which is an older version. — [FAR Online](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/f/rr_forenkladfaktura/) (secondary), [Bokio](https://www.bokio.se/blogg/forenklad-faktura/) (secondary)
- Simplified invoice minimum content: date, seller identification, type of goods/services, VAT amount or information to calculate it; credit notes refer to the original. — same FAR source
- Reverse-charge invoices: Skatteverket accepts "omvänd skattskyldighet" as alternative to ML wording "omvänd betalningsskyldighet". — same FAR source via search summary
- Skatteverket invoice page on invoice rules: [momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momslagensregleromfakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html) — surfaced in search but not read.

### Inferences
- IT consulting services to Swedish customers are 25% VAT.

## Momsrutor (SKV 4700) and reverse charge on foreign services

### Takeaway
Reverse charge on services from abroad: report the purchase base in ruta 21 (EU) or 22 (non-EU), self-assessed output VAT in ruta 30/31/32 by rate, and the matching input VAT in ruta 48. Both sides must be shown; no net-off ("tyst kvittning"). AWS, Google, GitHub, Atlassian are normally this case if the supplier is foreign and invoices without Swedish VAT.

### Cited Findings
- Ruta 21 = services purchased from another EU country; ruta 22 = services purchased from outside the EU; ruta 24 = other services bought in Sweden with buyer liable. — [Skatteverket, omvänd betalningsskyldighet](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/omvandskattskyldighet.4.47eb30f51122b1aaad28000258292.html) (fetched)
- Output VAT is self-calculated and reported in ruta 30, 31 or 32 depending on rate; input VAT deducted under ordinary rules in ruta 48; both must be reported, no net-off. — same source
- If the foreign seller wrongly charged VAT, reverse charge still applies on the full invoice amount including the wrong VAT; the incorrect VAT cannot be deducted; request a credit note and new invoice. — same source
- Ruta 23 relates to goods purchased in Sweden with buyer liable (domestic reverse charge). — [Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/vad-ar-omvand-skattskyldighet) via search summary (secondary; rutnumbers from older 2015/2016 SKV 4700 forms, verify)
- Ruta 39 = services sold to a taxable person in another EU country (main rule), reported in SEK, and the sale also goes in periodisk sammanställning with the buyer's VAT number. If the buyer's VAT number is missing but the buyer is shown to be taxable, include in ruta 39 but not in the sammanställning. — [Skatteverket, sälja tjänster till andra EU-länder](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagorganisationer/moms/momsvidhandelmedeulander/momsvidinternationelltjanstehandel.4.76a43be412206334b89800010696.html); [Björn Lundén](https://bjornlunden.com/se/bjornkoll/blogg/vad-ar-periodisk-sammanstallning/) (secondary)
- Periodisk sammanställning corrections: submit a new sammanställning for the same period asap. — [Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/rattaenperiodisksammanstallning.4.7856a2b411550b99fb7800088048.html) (search summary)
- OSS: threshold EUR 10,000 (SEK 99,680) total across all member states for telecom, broadcasting, electronic services and distance sales of goods to non-taxable persons in other EU states; below it Swedish VAT is charged; above it destination-country VAT, via OSS or local registration. Introduced 1 July 2021. — [Skatteverket, tröskelvärde](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/handelmedandralander/omsattningstroskelviddistansforsaljninginomeu.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80005545.html) (search summary)

### Inferences
- Foreign SaaS: a US supplier (AWS, GitHub, Atlassian via Irish/Dutch/Australian entities; Google Ireland) invoicing a Swedish AB B2B without VAT => ruta 21 if the supplier is an EU entity (e.g. Google Cloud EMEA Ireland, Atlassian BV Netherlands), ruta 22 if outside EU (e.g. GitHub Inc. US, AWS if billed by US entity). Check the invoicing entity per invoice. This is an inference from the cited rules; the invoicing entities are not verified here.
- Reverse-charged VAT on services is booked as a zero-net entry if the AB has full deduction rights (an IT consultancy normally does).
- EU service sales to business customers (e.g. consulting to a German company) go to ruta 39 plus periodisk sammanställning; sales to non-EU customers are outside Swedish VAT scope and go to ruta 40 (UNVERIFIED, see Gaps).

## Representation, deductions, foreign currency, bad debts, corrections

### Takeaway
Meal representation: VAT deductible on a base of max SEK 300 ex VAT per person/occasion (secondary sources); gifts max SEK 300 ex VAT. Corrections to filed returns are made by redoing the whole return in the e-service; correction possible up to 6 years. Bad debts: VAT relief on a confirmed customer loss in the period it is confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- External representation meals: VAT deductible on base up to SEK 300 ex VAT per person/occasion; simple fika about SEK 60; a simplified method of SEK 46 VAT per person when cost exceeds 300. — [FAR Online](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rm_representation/), [Spiris](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/representation) (secondary; via search summary)
- Representation gifts: VAT deductible up to SEK 300 ex VAT. — [Skatteverket entryscape](https://skatteverket.entryscape.net/store/9/resource/1927) (via summary)
- Meals for income tax: not deductible since 2017. — FAR as above
- 2026 employee meal benefit values (SEK 310/day full board, SEK 124 lunch/dinner) are for kostförmån, not representation. — [Skatteverket kostförmån](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/lonochersattning/formaner/kostforman)
- Corrections: redo the whole declaration and resubmit; can be done in accounting system or the e-service "Lämna momsdeklaration"; signed and submitted. A correction is possible up to 6 years after the period (Bokio, secondary). — [Skatteverket, rätta en momsdeklaration](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/rattaenmomsdeklaration.4.3684199413c956649b552c4.html); [Bokio](https://www.bokio.se/hjalp/moms-skatt/moms/ratta-momsdeklaration/)
- Bad debts (kundförluster): relief claimed in the return for the period the loss is confirmed, same VAT rate as original invoice, original invoice required; bankruptcy, ackord or utmätning are typical; later payment must be re-reported. A simplified route (3 months after due date, written reminder, no connection between parties) is described by Forvis Mazars (2020, may be outdated). — [Skatteverket, kundförluster](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/kundforlusteromkundenintekanbetala.4.5c1163881590be297b58d10.html); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyhetsbrev-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/momshantering-vid-kundfoerluster) (secondary)
- Svenskt Näringsliv (2025) argues the rules are applied restrictively and asks for law change (position, not law). — [Svenskt Näringsliv](https://www.svensktnaringsliv.se/english/xc5l0v_250422-hemstallan-forbattra-momsreglerna-vid-kundforluster-klar-w_1230929.html/250422+Hemsta%25CC%2588llan+fo%25CC%2588rba%25CC%2588ttra+momsreglerna+vid+kundfo%25CC%2588rluster+klar+webb.pdf)

## How to file: e-service, file upload, API, sandbox

### Takeaway
Three routes: manual entry in Skatteverket's e-service "Lämna momsdeklaration"; upload of an XML file in the same e-service; and the new API Momsdeklaration (live since November 2025) that prepares drafts in the company's own space, with final signing in Skatteverket's e-service. A complete test service exists in the Developer Portal; access requires an application form and API keys.

### Cited Findings
- In the e-service Lämna momsdeklaration you upload an XML file or enter figures manually; the XML must follow a specific structure described on "Lämna momsdeklaration via fil i e-tjänsten". — [Skatteverket, Deklarera moms](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms) (via search summary)
- Skatteverket recommends submitting from accounting software with API. The VAT-registered business or ombud can prepare the underlay and transfer it directly. — same source
- API Momsdeklaration went live in November 2025; Fortnox went live with it in February 2026. Users create/handle VAT return data in their own software and retrieve submitted returns and decided figures. Final signing happens on Skatteverket's side. — [Fortnox](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/driva-foretag/skatt/forenkla-momsdeklarationen-med-skatteverkets-nya-api) (fetched)
- Skatteverket lists API Momsdeklaration as a "komplett testtjänst" and a production service in the Developer Portal. — [Skatteverket, utvecklingsområde moms](https://skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/utvecklingsomraden/moms.4.7c708f0e16bed42cd0553bd.html) (fetched; page text still says "just nu utvecklar" and is partly stale)
- Event-log operation postponed to later. — [Skatteverket mötesanteckningar 18 Mar 2025](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/utvecklingsomraden/moms/motesloggar/moteden18mars2025.5.25688d121956e35b0c223de.html)
- Authorization: OAuth2. Options: Authorization Code Grant with personal e-legitimation (BankID, Test BankID for testing) or organisation certificate (ACG and CCG); the organisationslegitimation is currently issued only by Expisoft (test certificate downloadable). Required roles for the user: firmatecknare, deklarationsombud, momsdeklarationsombud or representative of enkelt bolag (the sources differ in wording). TLS 1.2. — [Börja använda Skatteverkets API:er](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/borjaanvandaapier.4.77f3e230191ef88bf1d585.html) (fetched); role list from [Tjänstebeskrivning Momsdeklaration v1.0](https://www7.skatteverket.se/portal-wapi/open/apier-och-oppna-data/utvecklarportalen/v1/getFile/tjanstebeskrivning-momsdeklaration-v1-0/pdf/1.0.4/Tjanstebeskrivning-Momsdeklaration-v1-0.pdf) (PDF unreadable via fetch; content only via search summary)
- Access: fill in a form, accept terms; test (complete test service) keys (client_id, client_secret) are emailed; production requires an agreement with Skatteverket and keys split across email and SMS; test and production keys must be separate. — [Börja använda API:er](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/borjaanvandaapier.4.77f3e230191ef88bf1d585.html) (fetched)
- Skatteverket's three-stage model: simple mockup (static, open to all via Developer Portal), complete test service (dynamic data), production. — [Skatteverket, anslutning och support](https://skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/anslutningochsupportforvaraapierochoppnadata.4.7c708f0e16bed42cd055bf0.html) (via search summary)
- General terms (Bilaga A, version 1.0, Nov 2025) apply to software companies with agreements; "Eget utrymme" is a protected area per VAT-registered company; software companies need not receive authorization directly from the end customer when digital ombud is used, and can view the digital ombud's authorizations via the Ombudshantering API. — [Allmänna villkor](https://www7.skatteverket.se/portal-wapi/open/apier-och-oppna-data/utvecklarportalen/v1/getFile/allmanna-villkor-momsdeklaration) (search summary; PDF unreadable); [Ny e-tjänst Digitalt ombud](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/prenumererapanyheterviarss/nyheteromdigitalasamarbeten/2026/nyhetsarkiv/nyetjanstdigitaltombud.5.3129d65419ef1497c0645d1.html) (not read)

Dated; verify. — [Skatteverket mötesanteckningar](https://www.skatteverket.se/omoss/digitalasamarbeten/utvecklingsomraden/moms/motesloggar/moteden10september2024.5.77f3e230191ef88bf1d129f.html)

## Penalties for late or incorrect filing

### Takeaway
Late return: förseningsavgift SEK 625 (once per occasion if several returns late). Incorrect/missing information: skattetillägg 20% of understated VAT; reduced rates for wrong-period errors; possible relief.

### Cited Findings
- Late filing fee SEK 625 if the deadline is missed. — [Skatteverket, När ska jag deklarera moms?](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html) (fetched); [Företagarna](https://www.foretagarna.se/juridisk-faq/skatter/forseningsavgifter-deklaration/) (secondary)
- Only one förseningsavgift if several returns are late at the same time; can be waived if unreasonable. — [FAR Online](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rs_forseningsavgift/) (via summary; secondary)
- Skattetillägg for VAT: 20% of the tax understated/overstated (40% and 10% apply to income tax). Wrong-period errors are lower (about 2% for up to 4 months, 5% for a full tax year, per Rättsserien). Can be waived fully or partly if unreasonable. — [Skatteverket, skattetillägg för företag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/skatter/betalaochfatillbaka/skattetillaggforforetag.4.2cf1b5cd163796a5c8bc24e.html) and [FAR](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/ratt-moms/s/rm_skattetillagg/) (via search summary)
- Interest: interest on unpaid VAT runs from the deadline (e.g., from 13 May for annual sole traders with bureau extension). — Skatteverket när ska jag deklarera (fetched)

### Inferences
- Correcting voluntarily before Skatteverket's review generally avoids skattetillägg for self-reported corrections (UNVERIFIED, see Gaps).
